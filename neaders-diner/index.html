We moved!

Formerly Neader's Cone N Shake out on the Pike, we're now at 333 Third Street in downtown Marietta. Same big menu, same everything-made-to-order, plus booths, a checkerboard floor and a whole lot of nostalgia.

Locally owned and operated, with prices that won't break the bank.
